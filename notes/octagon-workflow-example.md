# Octagon Financial Analyst Workflow Example

This is an example of how to use the Financial Analyst Master skill to analyze a company (e.g., NVDA).

## Phase 1: Data Collection
Retrieve real-time income statement data for NVDA
Retrieve detailed balance sheet data for NVDA
Retrieve cash flow statement data for NVDA
Retrieve analyst Revenue and EPS estimates for NVDA
Retrieve Altman Z-Score and Piotroski Score for NVDA

## Phase 2: Growth Analysis
Retrieve year-over-year growth in key income-statement items for NVDA (last 5 years)
Retrieve YoY growth in Total Assets, Liabilities, Equity for NVDA
Retrieve YoY growth in Operating Cash Flow and Free Cash Flow for NVDA
Retrieve historical financial ratings for NVDA (last 2000 records)

## Phase 3: Segmentation
Retrieve revenue breakdown by product segment for NVDA
Retrieve revenue breakdown by geographic segment for NVDA

## Phase 4: ESG
Retrieve ESG ratings and scores for NVDA
Retrieve ESG benchmark comparison for Technology sector for FY2024

## Phase 5: Peer Comparison (Repeat for AMD and INTC)
Retrieve year-over-year growth in key income-statement items for AMD
Retrieve year-over-year growth in key income-statement items for INTC
[... repeat other phases for peers ...]

## Phase 6: Report Generation
Synthesize findings into an equity research report with sections:
- Executive Summary & Snapshot
- Investment Thesis (3 bullets)
- Investment Positives
- Competitive/Peer Analysis
- Estimates & Operating Assumptions
- Valuation
- Key Risks
- ESG Assessment
- Appendix

Each section should footnote every number with source and date.
