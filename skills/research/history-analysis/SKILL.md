# History Analysis Skill

**Use when:** The user wants academic analysis of historical events, synthesis of time periods, evaluation of historical causes and effects, or contextual understanding of past events.

**Description:** This skill provides structured approaches to understanding history as a discipline — using primary sources, historical methodology, and evidence-based analysis rather than myth or propaganda.

## Capabilities

### 1. Historical Period Analysis
- Synthesize key characteristics of historical eras (ancient, classical, medieval, early modern, modern)
- Identify defining features, major transitions, and turning points
- Analyze cultural, political, and economic patterns within periods

### 2. Event Analysis
- Break down specific historical events into causes, conditions, and consequences
- Evaluate multiple historical interpretations
- Assess short-term and long-term impacts

### 3. Historical Figure Assessment
- Contextualize figures within their historical moment
- Balance achievements and controversies
- Analyze legacy and historical memory

### 4. Historical Methodology
- Critically evaluate primary vs. secondary sources
- Identify bias, perspective, and presentism in historical writing
- Assess historical consensus vs. revisionist views

### 5. Thematic Synthesis
- Connect events across time and geography
- Identify recurring patterns in human history
- Analyze continuity and change over time

## Methodologies

### Academic Approach
- **Source criticism**: Evaluate reliability, bias, and perspective of sources
- **Contextualization**: Situate events in their specific historical moment
- **Comparative analysis**: Compare similar events across different contexts
- **Multi-causal analysis**: Identify multiple contributing factors to events

### Source Types
- **Primary sources**: Contemporary documents, artifacts, eyewitness accounts
- **Secondary sources**: scholarly analysis, textbooks, historical surveys
- **Tertiary sources**: Encyclopedias, datababases, reference works

## Limitations

- **Not propaganda**: Does not promote any nationalist, religious, or ideological agenda
- **Not mythological**: Distinguishes between historical evidence and legend
- **Not predictive**: Cannot predict future based on past patterns
- **Complexity**: History is always more nuanced than simplified narratives

## Example Uses

```bash
# Analyze causes of the French Revolution
/history-analysis french-revolution

# Synthesize the Renaissance period
/history-analysis renaissance

# Evaluate historical legacy of a figure
/history-analysis alexander-the-great

# Compare revolutions across time
/history-analysis compare-revolutions
```

## Principles

1. **Evidence-based**: Conclusions follow from available sources, not assumptions
2. **Multi-perspective**: Consider diverse voices and experiences within history
3. **Avoid presentism**: Judge actions by standards of their time, not ours
4. **Intellectual honesty**: Acknowledge gaps in knowledge and conflicting interpretations
