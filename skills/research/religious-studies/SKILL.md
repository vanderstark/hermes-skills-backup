# Religious Studies Skill

**Use when:** The user wants academic study and comparison of religions, religious history, theology, rituals, scriptures, or religious literacy.

**Description:** This skill provides structured approaches to understanding religions as cultural, historical, and social phenomena — without promoting any specific faith or claiming truth claims.

## Capabilities

### 1. Comparative Religion
- Compare core concepts across traditions (God/Ultimate Reality, afterlife, salvation, moral codes)
- Map similarities and differences in rituals, practices, and scriptures
- Analyze historical development of religious movements

### 2. Religious History
- Timeline key events in religious traditions
- Identify founder figures and their historical contexts
- Trace spread and adaptation of beliefs across regions

### 3. Scripture Literacy
- Overview of major religious texts (Bible, Quran, Vedas, Tripitaka, etc.)
- Understand genre, authorship, and historical context
- Identify central themes and moral teachings

### 4. Religious sociology
- Role of religion in society and culture
- Religious transformation and modernization
- Interfaith dynamics and dialogue

### 5. Philosophical Theology
- Arguments for and against God's existence
- Problem of evil, free will, determinism
- Religious experience and epistemology

## Methodologies

### Academic Approach
- **Historical-critical method**: Analyze texts in their original historical context
- **Comparative methodology**: Systematic comparison across traditions
- **Phenomenology of religion**: Describe religious experience without evaluation
- **Sociological analysis**: Study religion as social phenomenon

### Sources
- Primary: Religious texts in translation, historical records, archaeology
- Secondary: Academic scholarship, theology, religious studies journals
- Tertiary: Encyclopedia, textbooks, reliable surveys

## Limitations

- **Not theological**: Does not answer "is this true?" questions
- **Not devotional**: Not for prayer, worship, or religious practice
- **Not proselytizing**: Does not promote one faith over another
- **Cultural lens**: Always approaches religion as cultural/historical phenomenon

## Example Uses

```bash
# Compare concepts of afterlife across traditions
/religious-studies compare-afterlife

# Timeline of major religious events
/religious-studies timeline

# Overview of Islamic prayer practices
/religious-studies islamic-practices

# Historical context of Buddha's life
/religious-studies buddhist-history
```

## Principles

1. **Academic neutrality**: Study religion as phenomenon, not truth claim
2. **Contextual understanding**: Always situate beliefs in historical/social context
3. **Textual literacy**: Acknowledge multiple interpretations of scriptures
4. **Respectful engagement**: Treat all traditions with academic respect
